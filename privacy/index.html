Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Tenanto is a rental management service operated by Omnatix in South Africa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we process, why, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). It covers the Tenanto landlord app, the tenant portal and this website.

1. Who is responsible for your information

  • Landlords: for your own account information, we are the responsible party.
  • Tenants: your landlord decides what information about you is captured in Tenanto and why, so your landlord is the responsible party. We act as an operator: we store and process that information only on your landlord's instructions and to run the service.
  • Website visitors: if you join the waitlist, we are the responsible party for your email address.

Our Information Officer can be reached at info@omnatix.co.za.

2. What we collect

From landlords

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and business name.
  • Banking details you choose to add, so your tenants know where to pay rent. Tenanto never moves money.
  • Your rental records: properties, units, leases, rent records, expenses, receipts, documents, inspections and maintenance tickets.

About tenants (entered by the landlord or by the tenant in the portal)

  • Name, phone number and email address.
  • South African ID or passport number, which is stored encrypted.
  • Lease details, rent records and proof of payment uploads.
  • Maintenance reports, including any photos the tenant attaches.
  • Signatures captured for leases and inspections, and inspection photos.

Technical information

  • Error reports when the app or portal crashes: the error message, app version and device platform. Portal links are removed from these reports.
  • A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications in the landlord app.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Why we use it

PurposeLawful basis (POPIA section 11)
Providing Tenanto: accounts, records, the tenant portal, documents and notifications Performance of our agreement with the landlord
Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fixing errors Our legitimate interest in a safe, working service
Helping landlords keep rental and tax records The landlord's legal obligations, including to SARS
Telling waitlist members when Tenanto launches Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Tenanto:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Expo, Google and Apple, which deliver push notifications to the landlord app.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and the tenant portal files.

Some of these providers store information on servers outside South Africa. Where that happens, we rely on providers bound by agreements and laws that give a level of protection comparable to POPIA, as section 72 requires. We will disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it.

Tenants: your landlord sees the information in their own account, including what you submit through your portal. Other landlords and other tenants cannot see it.

5. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Database access rules ensure each landlord can only reach their own records. ID numbers are encrypted in the database.
  • Files such as receipts, leases and photos are private and shared only through links that expire.
  • Each tenant portal link is unique and secret. A landlord can replace a link at any time, which switches the old one off.

If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA section 22 requires.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and rental records are kept while the landlord's account is open. When a landlord deletes their account, their records and files are deleted straight away.
  • Error reports are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until launch, or until you ask us to remove yours.

Landlords must keep rental records for SARS for at least five years. Please export or download what you need before deleting your account.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask whether we hold information about you, and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, out of date or no longer needed;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Landlords can edit their details in the app and delete their account under Settings. Tenants should contact their landlord first, because the landlord controls their records. You can also email us at info@omnatix.co.za and we will help, or pass your request to the right landlord. See also how to delete your account.

8. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. The tenant portal saves your language choice and your portal link on your own device, so it opens straight to your portal next time. The landlord app keeps you signed in by storing a session on your phone.

9. Children

Tenanto is meant for adults. Landlord accounts are for people aged 18 or older.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell landlords in the app or by email.
