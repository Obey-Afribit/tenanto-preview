Questions landlords ask

Do my tenants need to download an app? +

No. You send each tenant a private link by WhatsApp, SMS or email, and it opens in their phone browser. Nothing to install, no password.

Is my data safe? +

Your records sit in a secure database with strict access rules: only you can see your properties and tenants. ID numbers are encrypted, files are private, and we follow POPIA.

Does Tenanto collect rent for me? +

No. Tenants pay you directly by EFT as they do today. They can send proof of payment through their portal, and you confirm it in the app once the money reflects.

What is the SARS audit pack? +

One PDF per property per tax year with your rental income, expenses grouped by SARS category and every receipt attached. Ready for your tax practitioner or an audit.

Does it work in Afrikaans and isiZulu? +

Yes. The landlord app and the tenant portal both work in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu.

Can I leave at any time? +

Yes. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings in the app.
