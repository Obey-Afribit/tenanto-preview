Delete your Tenanto account

You can delete your Tenanto account and all of its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Tenanto app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the page.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap the red button.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Cannot use the app?

Email info@omnatix.co.za from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Tenanto account". We will confirm the request with you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile and banking details.
  • All properties, units, tenants, leases, rent records, expenses, maintenance tickets, inspections and documents in your account.
  • All files: receipts, lease documents, photos and signatures.
  • Your tenants' portal links, which stop working immediately.

Error reports that do not identify you are kept for up to 12 months and are then deleted automatically.

Before you delete

SARS expects landlords to keep rental records for at least five years. Download your SARS audit packs and any lease documents you need before you delete your account.

Tenants

Tenants do not have a Tenanto account. Your landlord controls your records, so ask your landlord to remove your information. If you cannot reach them, email us at info@omnatix.co.za and we will help. See our Privacy Policy for your rights.
